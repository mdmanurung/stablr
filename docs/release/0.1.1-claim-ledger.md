## Status vocabulary

- **SUPPORTED** — backed by a preserved, hash-verified artifact, and the claim
  as written matches what that artifact says.
- **QUALIFIED** — backed, but the claim overstates it. The required rewording is
  given.
- **WITHDRAWN** — no preserved promotable evidence. Removed from the text.
- **UNVERIFIED** — asserted in prose with no artifact at all.

## Claims that are now supported but were not before

These are additions the evidence now permits, and they are the honest core of
the paper:

- The MVR S-matrix solver previously could return an **all-zero** `S` while
  reporting `knockoff_mvr` with an empty fallback history, putting six
  zero-valued decoys into the FDP+ calibration. Reproduced and fixed.
- An explicit `hard_threshold` silently overrode the computed FDP+ threshold.
  The FDP+ curve was returned but never influenced selection. Now suppressed and
  recorded.
- Permutation decoys inherited their source feature's sparse-group assignment,
  so the artificial block did not face a comparable penalty structure — a direct
  confound on the FDP+ comparison. Now opt-in and off by default.
- The inner-CV candidate comparison was unpaired, injecting bootstrap noise into
  a single-number selection rule. Now uses common random numbers.
- Adaptive lasso silently reduced to plain lasso on small-scale responses.

None of these should appear in the manuscript as novelty. They belong in the
package's own release notes and in reviewer responses, and their value to the
paper is that they make the reported numbers trustworthy.

## Still required before submission

1. **A calibration study** supporting or refuting "FDP+ control". This is the
   only route to restoring the stronger claim.
2. **Revision of the OOL protocol's Spearman and Jaccard gates**, which are
   above the measured noise floor.
3. **A statement of the estimand** in Methods: what FDP+ controls, over which
   grid, and with what multiplicity policy. The package's own gate is bounded to
   a 96-cell design matrix, which is not a universal guarantee.
4. **Author list, affiliations, ORCIDs, funding, acknowledgments, declaration of
   interests** (`SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md:5-10`).
